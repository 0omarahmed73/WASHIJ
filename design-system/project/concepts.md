# Creative exploration

Three distinct directions were drawn and judged against four tests: does it read at 16px, does it say "connection" without a literal icon, does it relate to painting, and can it generate a graphic language without the logo.

| | Thread and Frame | Warp | Nuqat |
|---|---|---|---|
| Idea | One thread crosses a portrait frame, over one edge and under the other | Eight warp lines; one thread leaves the field | Three dots of the letter ش, joined by lines |
| Reads at 16px | Yes | Weak (becomes a barcode) | Yes |
| Connected to painting | Yes (the frame) | No | No |
| Generates a pattern | Yes (the Long Thread) | Yes | Weak |
| Risk | Resembles a link icon if the over/under is lost | Generic | Looks like a network diagram or a start-up |

**Selected: Thread and Frame (الخيط والإطار).** The Warp concept informs the rhythm of the thread pattern; the Nuqat concept was dropped.

Why it works: the frame is the painting, the thread is the story. The over and under crossing means the story enters the work and leaves it for another. It needs no illustration, and it stays recognisable when only the Long Thread is visible and no logo is present.

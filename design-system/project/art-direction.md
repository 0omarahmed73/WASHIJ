# Art direction

The artwork is the hero. The identity frames it; it never competes with it.

- **Sources.** Images come from the holding museum or a verified public-domain source, with a credit line. Never alter hue, contrast or saturation to fit the page.
- **Hero compositions.** Full work visible, uncropped, hung on `bg` with `shadow-hang`. One thread may cross the composition once; it never crosses a face.
- **Thumbnails.** 4:5 for portrait works, 3:2 for landscape, no overlay. Title and artist below, not on the image.
- **Artist portraits.** Original tonality. If no portrait exists, use no portrait: set the name large and let a work stand for the artist.
- **Detail crops.** Always labelled with the whole work, framed with the corner-mark device, never cropped through a signature.
- **Editorial illustration.** Only our own thread and frame drawings, in flat ink and yellow. No stock imagery.
- **Backgrounds.** Dark gallery interiors or our own stone texture under a 70-75% `bg` scrim, never a painting.
- **Atmosphere.** Directional, low-key light with a deep ground; no flare, no vignette on the paintings themselves.

# Brand guidelines

## 1. Concept and positioning
WASHIJ is a carefully curated digital museum where visitors discover artworks and the stories, people and historical connections behind them. It is not a marketplace, an auction house or an e-commerce brand. Personality: poetic but not sentimental; mysterious but not dark for its own sake; classical but not old-fashioned; luxurious through restraint; intellectual yet approachable.

## 2. Logo construction and variations
Frame 54 x 90 (3:5) on a 120 grid. Thread 108 x 76, 35 degrees, over the left edge and under the right. Frame and thread share one stroke, 3.2; the perpendicular clearance at each crossing equals one stroke, and the thread overhangs the frame by the same 27 units on both sides. At 32px and below use stroke 5; at 16px use 7. Nine family members are shown in LogoVariants.

## 3. Clear space and minimum sizes
Clear space x = half the symbol width. Minimum: symbol 16px (6mm print), Arabic wordmark 72px (20mm), English wordmark 80px (22mm), horizontal lockup 160px (40mm).

## 4. Incorrect usage
No stretching, rotating, recolouring, effects, tracked Arabic, outlined or detached threads, and no placement on detailed imagery. See LogoRules.

| mist | #9FB3CF | Secondary text on dark |
| star-yellow | #F2C94C | The thread; one primary button |
| ivory | #F6F2E9 | Primary text; symbol frame |

Evaluated, not copied: the six brief values are kept (midnight, indigo, cobalt, muted sky, star yellow, ivory) and completed with surfaces and text partners so every pair passes. Cobalt is 3.3:1 on midnight, so it fills controls but never carries text. Yellow is the thread, never text, and covers under 5 percent of any composition.

## 6. Typography hierarchy

## 9. Layout principles
- RTL first: the Arabic reading column sits on the right, the artwork on the left; mirror for English.
- 12-column grid, 48px gutters on desktop, 16px on mobile.
- One image, one headline, one thread per composition.
- Generous empty ground; text never over painting detail.
- Max line length: 60 characters Arabic reading text, 70 English.

## 10. Applications
Homepage (featured artwork, collections, artists, movements), ArtworkDetail (whole work, context, attributed interpretations, sources, related), ArtistHeader, Collection, AppIcons, Poster, EditorialCover, InfoCard, MuseumPass.

## 11. Accessibility and contrast
Body text meets 4.5:1 in both themes (the Contrast card lists every pair). Borders and focus rings meet 3:1. Never use colour alone for state; error text carries a word. Minimum Arabic body size 18px. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`; the identity needs no motion.

## 12. Arabic and English usage
- Arabic leads; the English wordmark appears beneath or after it, never above.
- Never letter-space Arabic. Never use uppercase for Arabic.
- Keep Latin names inside Arabic text as isolated LTR runs; in headings keep Latin and Arabic at the same size.
- Use Western digits throughout.
- Vocalise the wordmark (the fatha on و) at 32px and above; omit the yellow fatha below 24px.
